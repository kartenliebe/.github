We're the online shop for everything that makes life's big moments personal: cards and invitations for weddings, births, birthdays and Christmas, matching stationery like place cards and menus, plus photo books, calendars and wall art. Customers design everything themselves in our online editor, and we print it in premium quality. Our own tech team builds the shop, the editor and the software behind it.

Kartenliebe is part of the [ALL IN GROUP](https://www.all-in-group.com), a leading e-commerce group. Under one roof it brings together brands like MYPOSTER, JUNIQE, Kartenliebe and ArtPhotoLimited, plus our own production, Printhouse. What connects us: a shared mindset, real teamplay and a high bar for **really good quality**.
